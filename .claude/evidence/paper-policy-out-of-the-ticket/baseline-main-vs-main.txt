# How the mask was derived, and the one scene that was flaky before the rebase

Two rounds of measurement, on two different bases. Both are kept because the
second alone would not explain where the mask came from, and a reviewer who
found only the first would reasonably ask why a scene diverged.

## Round 1 - before the rebase, base d551813

Two runs of the SAME origin/main build, tape drained, and the comparison below.
Eight of nine scenes matched outside the mask; `paper_strategy_editor` did not.
That is what the mask is FOR: this round is what showed that the divergence
between two identical builds is confined to rows 0-30 and 662-672, and that one
scene could still wobble outside them on a tape that had not fully settled.

## Round 2 - after the rebase onto d3cf317, and the run the PR reports

The same fixture on the rebased base, with the hold long enough for the tape to
drain completely. Here `paper_strategy_editor` is stable too, so the control is
9/9 and the branch matches it 9/9. This is the round `pixels-golden.txt`
reports, and the round the claim rests on.

The two rounds disagree about that one scene because the first did not let the
tape finish; nothing about the branch changed between them - round 1 compared
main against main, and so did round 2.
