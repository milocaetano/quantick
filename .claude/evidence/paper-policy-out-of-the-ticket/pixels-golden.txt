# Pixels: what this fixture can and cannot prove

Read this before trusting a number in it.

## The honest summary

The pixel criterion asked for captures that are identical between `origin/main`
and this branch. **This fixture cannot deliver that reliably, and the control
runs are what say so.** Every claim below is stated against its own control:
two runs of the SAME `origin/main` build, captured the same way. Where the
control does not match itself, the branch cannot be asked to match it either.

Two independent inputs move under the capture and neither is the tape:

1. **The clock and the tape position**, painted in two bands - rows 0-30 and
   662-672. Those are masked, and the mask was measured rather than chosen.
2. **The depth book.** It arrives from a live venue, independently of the
   replay recording, so one run shows a book and the next shows "no book" -
   a 58x200 px block of the chart, worth about 0.15% of the frame. This was
   found by cropping the region two runs disagreed on and looking at it.
   `QUANTICK_FEED_STALL=silent` pins it empty, but stalling introduces its own
   timing and the control gets *worse*, not better.

## The numbers, each against its control

| Round | Control (main vs main) | Branch vs main |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tape drained, book unpinned | 9/9 | 9/9, twice |
| Tape drained, book unpinned, later | 9/9 | 4/9 and 6/9 - the book had arrived |
| Book pinned with the stall hook | 6/9 | 7/9 against each control run |

The first row is the one the branch was first measured on and it is real, but
the second row shows it was not repeatable: the same branch build scored 9/9
and then 4/9 against a control that never moved. In the third row the branch
matches main on **every scene where main matches itself**, which is the most
this fixture can honestly assert.

## What that means for the claim

"Pixel-identical" is **not proven** here, and this file says so rather than
reporting the round that happened to be flattering. What is proven, by
evidence that does not move:

- `the_journal_bytes_are_fixed` - the money path's output, byte for byte, same
  SHA-256 before and after the split, in CI on every run.
- `scene-compare.txt` - the control plane's own answer for what is on screen:
  4/4 scenes identical across 64-84 stable fields per scene, with only
  `instance_id`, `captured_at_unix_ms` and `capture_elapsed_us` differing.
- 1,937 tests, 206 of them paper, against 1,935 and 204 on `origin/main`.

A future run of this fixture should pin the book properly - a config with no
reachable venue rather than the stall hook - before its numbers are quoted.
