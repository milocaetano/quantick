# Pixels: four rounds of trying, and what the fixture can actually prove

Read this before quoting a number from it. The short version: **the pixel
criterion is not met, and not because of the branch.**

## What was tried, and what each round surfaced

Every round is stated against its own control - two runs of the SAME
`origin/main` build, captured identically. Where the control does not match
itself, the branch cannot be asked to match it either.

| Round | Fixture | Control (main vs main) | Branch vs main |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | tape drained | 9/9 | 9/9, twice |
| 2 | same, later | 9/9 | 4/9, then 6/9 |
| 3 | `QUANTICK_FEED_STALL=silent` | 6/9 | 7/9 |
| 4 | offline config, no reachable feed | 8/9 | 7/9 and 6/9 |

Round 1 looked like proof and was not: the same branch build scored 9/9 and
then 4/9 against a control that never moved. Each round after it was an
attempt to remove one uncontrolled input, and each one surfaced another.

## The uncontrolled inputs, in the order they were found

1. **The clock and the tape position** - two horizontal bands, rows 0-30 and
   662-672, measured from the control rather than chosen. Masked. Round 4
   showed the top band actually reaches row 31 in one scene, so even the mask
   was a row short.
2. **The depth book.** It comes from whichever venue the default feed dials,
   not from the replay recording, so one run paints a book and the next says
   "no book" - a 58x200 px block, found by cropping the region two runs
   disagreed on and looking at it. `offline.toml` points the only feed at a
   port nothing connects to and names no `bridge_command`; it reduced the
   problem and did not remove it.
3. **The window size.** `offMain2-paper_order_hover.png` came back
   2576x1408 where every other capture is 1116x689 - one launch did not take
   `__COMPAT_LAYER=DPIUNAWARE`. A capture at a different size is not a
   different picture, it is a broken one, and it read as a control failure.

## The verdict

**A10 is not met.** Three independent inputs move under this fixture and the
mission's own control run is the thing that says so. No number here should be
quoted as evidence that the branch changed nothing on screen.

What carries that claim instead, all of it deterministic and re-runnable:

- `the_journal_bytes_are_fixed` - the money path's whole output, byte for
  byte, same SHA-256 before and after the split and after a rebase.
- `scene-compare.txt` - the control plane's own answer for what is on screen,
  4/4 scenes identical across 64-84 stable fields each, with only
  `instance_id`, `captured_at_unix_ms` and `capture_elapsed_us` differing.
- 1,937 tests, 206 of them paper, against 1,935 and 204 on `origin/main`.
- `health-compare.txt` - fps and frame CPU under a dense tape, flat.

A fixture that could support the claim needs the book pinned at the source and
the window size asserted per capture before the shutter. That is a mission of
its own, and it is named as a follow-up rather than pretended away here.
