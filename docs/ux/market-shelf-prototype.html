Test script

  1. 1Click the BTCUSDT card on Home. A tab opens.
  2. 2Go back through the Home house icon, always the first tab. The card got the “Open” badge and the “My markets” row appeared.
  3. 3Click BTCUSDT again: it focuses the tab that already exists, without duplicating.
  4. 4Press + (in the app: Ctrl+T; here also Ctrl+K), type “eth” and press Enter.
  5. 5In the +, type “doge”: the market is not in yours, Enter adds and opens it.
  6. 6In the +, type “win”: MT5 shows its real status and the replay appears separately.
  7. 7Ctrl+click a card (or Ctrl+Enter in the +) opens it in the background.
  8. 8Right-click a chart tab.
  9. 9On Home, hover a card and click the ☆, or use the + Add to Home card. The market stays pinned in “My markets”.
  10. 10Click the × on any card to remove it from Home. It stays in search; “Show hidden” brings it back.

Prices, trades and the brokers' symbol lists are simulated. Interface text is in English, as in the app.

Build steps

Five deliveries, each usable on its own

The order follows what you asked: first the Market Shelf, then opening new tabs. Each phase becomes a testable PR and does not depend on the later ones.

Phase 1S

Home as a fixed tab

The Market Shelf becomes the ⌂ tab, always first and never closed.

  • A card opens a tab; if already open, focuses it
  • “Open” badge on the card
  • “Home” house icon always visible at the start of the tab bar
  • Sparklines stop while Home is hidden
Phase 2M

“Open a market” in the +

The current broker and symbol window becomes the centered search. It reuses the add-symbol flow and the catalog's refusal.

  • Headless market list, shared with Home
  • Grouped by asset, one row per broker
  • “open · tab 3” focuses the existing tab
  • Ctrl+Enter opens in the background
Phase 3M

Home your way

You choose what appears. Home grows with a single row, without becoming a wall of cards.

  • ☆ pins, × removes, “+ Add to Home” adds
  • Pinned first, then recent, max 8
  • Home list saved in the workspace, not in feeds.toml
  • Second launch restores the tabs; with no tabs, opens on Home
  • A failing feed: “Feed unavailable · Retry · Back to Home”
Phase 4M

Markets beyond the cards

Search also lists what the broker has and you have not added yet.

  • Binance and Hyperliquid symbol list, fetched by the feed crate
  • No price until it starts streaming
  • MT5 with real status; replay by date
  • Asset set in the config, no guessing
Phase 5M

Tab in its own window

For the second monitor, or side by side with Win+← / Win+→. Solves WIN × WDO without building a split.

  • “Move to new window” in the tab menu
  • Closing the window returns the tab
  • Measure CPU with two windows before shipping
LaterL

Split with several markets

2 or 2×2 markets in the same tab. Today the panes of a tab all share one market, so this touches per-pane feeds and the workspace format. Only if side-by-side windows work badly in real use.

Where the designers disagreed and what I decided
  • What the + opens: a centered window over the chart, not Home. Whoever presses + wants to type a ticker; Home is one click away through the “Browse all in Home ⌂” link.
  • Shortcut: Ctrl+T, which already exists. Ctrl+K as an alias.
  • Ctrl+Enter: one designer wanted “force a second tab”, another “background”. Background wins; duplicate goes to the tab menu.
  • Shift+Enter: reserved for “open in new window” in phase 5. Replay appears as its own row in search, with a REPLAY badge.
  • You build Home: ☆ pins a market in “My markets” in the order you choose, × removes any card (including the starting ones), and “+ Add to Home” opens search in add mode. Recent ones come after the pinned. Removing from Home never removes from search.
  • Grouping BTCUSDT, BTC and BTCUSD: only through an asset key in the config. Guessing would merge WIN and WINV26 under one name, which hides a real difference.
