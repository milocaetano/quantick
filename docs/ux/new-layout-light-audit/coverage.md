## Navigation reconciliation

All 32 route groups have been visited at the scope of their exposed menu/configuration surfaces. Later reviewed captures179–187 close indicator categories/search/no-match/source choices, the actual candle time-axis menu, Tick settings and Default workspace protection. This is **32 groups visited, not 32 groups passed**.

Remaining sampled-state limits include populated recent-files list, other drawing-object-specific inspectors, preset mutations and destructive Delete/Reset confirmations. These are explicitly unexecuted; they do not imply that the actual empty/disabled menu surfaces were skipped. Layer-menu recovery is observed: near-bottom189 is close to the edge, but reopening higher190 exposes the full list through candle aggression. Populated EMA legend collapse is inspected in191. No requirement to exhaust every numeric setting, market condition or object property permutation is imposed.

## Explicit limits and observed outcomes

The saved-desk feedback and enabled Reset startup row were observed, but reset was not executed. Native Export and Import dialogs were opened and cancelled; the attempted export had invalid native path input, so no saved export or imported content is claimed. Native Windows dark file dialogs are outside Quantick theme ownership.

Replay playback used a clearly named synthetic fixture. Replay selection, loading, playing, transport, Session details and closing are covered; this does not establish real historical market accuracy or broker download behavior. Paper ticket alternatives were inspected without submitting orders. Populated trades, live financial execution, connected-client permission revocation, authentication failures and service errors were not exercised.

Settings fit at the inspected 1000-pixel width. Repeated no-match market-footer overlap and Top-toolbar black gutter remain findings, not passes. Top gutter is visible154/157, absent in Bottom155/Hidden156/Left163. Drawing-manager toolbar collision112–114 and low-emphasis annotation text108/110 remain separately documented findings.

This reviewer changed documentation/evidence records only. No source or runtime implementation was edited. The audit does not claim fixes, pixel-perfect compliance, complete keyboard behavior, accessibility certification, or every persistence path.
