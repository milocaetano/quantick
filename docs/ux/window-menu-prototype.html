Quantick · Market Shelf · tabs, window menu and workspace per tab

Every tab is a market, and each market opens on a workspace you choose and can see.

Tabs live in the title bar, as in Chrome; a tab dragged off becomes its own window. What belongs to the window sits under one ⋮ at the right. Each tab names its workspace beside the market; the "this tab" row under the tabs is where you switch, save and revert. Three reviewers (information architect, UX researcher with the app's personas, interaction designer) judged the design; their verdicts and disagreements are at the end. Prices, trades and venue lists are simulated.

Test script

  1. 1Look at the tab row: every tab names its workspace in grey. WINZ26 is on Scalp, BTC on Swing.
  2. 2Click Scalp ▾ on the "this tab" row and pick Tape. Only this tab changes; the toast says "drawings kept · Undo".
  3. 3Click + CVD: the tab gets a •. WDOZ26, which also uses Tape, did not change.
  4. 4Press Save (or Ctrl+S): the bar names the tabs that will change before you confirm. "Save as new…" creates a workspace for this tab alone.
  5. 5Right-click a tab: "Workspace ▸" lists the same names, with ✓ on the current one.
  6. 6+: under the list, two labelled selectors, Bars and Workspace. Move down to WINZ26: Bars switches to tick 2000 by itself, the bar that market remembers. Set Workspace to Tape and open eth. What you changed turns amber.
  7. 7Type btc: BTC is already open, so the button says "Focus tab 2". With Workspace on Scalp it becomes "Open second tab"; tab 2 never changes under you.
  8. 8⋮ or F10: "Save to Tape" and "Save desk" are separate rows with distinct shortcuts; Workspace ▸ and Desk ▸ are different submenus.
  9. 9Drag the BTCUSDT tab down and release: its own window, same workspace and drawings.
  10. 10Ctrl+Shift+P: every command above appears by name, with {tab} where it needs one.

The three reviewers: where they agreed, where they split, what was decided

Consensus (3 of 3)

  • Drawings never travel with the workspace. Today ChartLayout keys drawings by pane index; switching BTC from Scalp to Swing would hide its levels. The trader's layouts.toml already shows the damage: WIN drawings split across WINV26 (13), WINZ26 (5) and WIN$N (1) at every roll.
  • Free the word "workspace". Today it names the whole cockpit. "Save workspace" cannot mean two things.
  • Panels belong to the window. A workspace that closes the paper panel while a position is open is the worst case.
  • The same market on another workspace opens a second tab, never re-skins the existing one silently; the palette says so before Enter.
  • On open, two labelled selectors, Bars and Workspace, under the list. Workspace is Default unless chosen (the trader's call: no guessing the last used). Bars follows the selected market: WIN remembers tick 2000, BTC tick 50. What was changed turns amber.
  • One command registry feeds the menu, the right-click and the palette; the snapshot gives {symbol, venue, workspace, modified} per tab for the bot.

Disagreements

  • Save model. IA: shared and live, with an Undo toast ("the strip already works that way, and he never saves a bookmark"). UX and designer: a copy on the tab with a • until "Save to X", naming the affected tabs ("changing BTC must not re-arrange WDO in a window he is trading from").
  • Where to show it. UX and designer: a suffix on the tab. IA: only in the "this tab" row ("tabs truncate; the chip is the market"). Designer's final verdict: the name on the tab as a label, the control on the row.
  • The window's name. UX and designer: Session. IA: Desk, because Session collides three times.
  • The per-pane strip (Fluxo / Force Bar Only). UX: one strip per tab. IA: rename to "Indicator sets". Designer: leave it.
  • Bookmarks. IA: retire them (zero saved in the trader's file). The other two kept them under another name.
Decided in this prototype
  • Copy on the tab, explicit save. 2 of 3, and the blocker's argument outweighs habit: a change that jumps to another chart mid-tape is worse than a forgotten amber dot. On exit the tab's copy comes back tomorrow still marked •; nothing enters a workspace without "Save to".
  • Name on the tab, control on the row. The tab answers "which workspace?" for 4+ tabs without a hover; the control outside the tab avoids the slipped click that becomes a drag. Both read one state, with a test that they agree.
  • Desk. No collisions. Ctrl+Shift+S stays "Save desk", the meaning the trader's fingers know; Ctrl+S, free until now, becomes "Save to <workspace>".
  • The per-pane strip stays out of this design. It becomes workspace content; renaming it is a separate decision.
  • Bookmarks retired. If they are ever missed, they come back as "Saved desks", not as workspaces.
Base decisions for the window
  • No Windows title bar. The window is born undecorated (egui with_decorations(false)); the tab row is the drag region, double-click maximizes, three buttons drawn by the app. Win+← / Win+→ keep working. One row less, given to the chart.
  • A torn-off tab becomes a window, as in Chrome. Same feed, workspace and drawings; closing the window brings the tab back. WIN and WDO side by side without building a split.
  • ⋮, not ≡ and not a gear. ≡ already lives on the tool rail and on the dock icons; a gear promises settings and hides Desk, Replay and Help. F10 opens it; Alt is the sell modifier in CMD trading and Alt+1…9 switches layouts.
  • Submenus open to the left, two levels at most. The menu sits on the right edge.
  • Settings is a window with sections (Display, Data, Recording, Agent access), not seven checkboxes in a hover menu.
  • "Open a market" palette. Three designers (visual, trading platforms, command palettes) asked to hide bars and workspace behind typed tokens (@scalp, 500t) and Ctrl+K. Tested and refused by the trader: "strange for a human to understand". What they also asked and does not depend on that stays: the bar is the market's (WIN tick 2000, BTC tick 50); the button says what Enter does ("Focus tab 2", "Open second tab"); commands only behind >; Recent first; proportional type, mono only on numbers; a visible selection. The two selectors stay visible and labelled under the list, as in TradingView's open-chart dialog.
  • Migration: on first launch each open tab becomes a workspace named after its source market ("from WINZ26"), identical recipes deduplicated; nothing changes on screen on day one. The trader renames them.
  • Cost: Market Shelf phase 1 (strip, ⋮, pill, clock) is S. Workspace per tab with drawings per market is the model change: migrate ~770 drawing lines to an asset + venue key, SavedTab gains a workspace and its own state, command aliases for one release.
