Bubble size: getting the black box off the chart

Today there are two black boxes floating over the candles and over the tape, each with a grey slider. The scale runs from 0,25× to 3×, default 1×, visual only. Four nice ways to do it; try each one.

Today

A · draggable number

Next to the bubbles icon, the value. Drag sideways; double-click returns to 1×. No track.

B · thin track

A 2 px line with a dot. The value only shows while you drag. The tick mark is 1×.

C · mouse wheel on the icon

No control in sight. Scroll the wheel over the bubbles icon; the value flashes for a second and disappears.

D · the sample bubble

The control is a bubble at the current size. Drag up and it grows, drag down and it shrinks. What you see is what you get.

How each one behaves

  • Where it lives: on the chart line, right after the bubbles icon, not over the candles. The black box goes away.
  • Two targets: today there is one slider for the candle bubbles and another for the tape bubbles. The line holds just one, which scales the candle bubbles; in the tape-only view, it scales the tape ones. The tape keeps its own on right-click.
  • Back to 1×: double-click in A, B and D; in C, click the wheel.
  • A and C are the cleanest. B is the most familiar. D is the clearest about what it does, and the most unusual.
  • In all of them, the bubble_scale.set command stays the same.
