Candles, tape, or both: the three views of the Flow chart

Two ways to control the same thing. Click the blue icons inside each chart.

A · two buttons, one for each part

Candles and Tape light up and switch off on their own. The last one lit cannot be switched off.

B · one button that cycles the three views

Each click moves to the next view. The icon shows which one is active.

The three views, still

Three more ways, less obvious

The difference

  • A shows the state at all times, and the tape alone is one click. Costs two icons.
  • B costs one icon. Tape only is two clicks from today's view, and you only learn the next view after clicking.
  • C is a thumbnail picker: the button is the drawing of the view. Reads without a caption, costs three icons.
  • D has no button: the tape becomes the control. Double-click on it to fill everything; double-click again to go back. Invisible to newcomers, so it needs one of the others alongside.
  • E is the divider with a handle: drag it to the edge and the other part disappears; a stroke on the edge brings it back. It is what the app already does today, only with a visible handle.
  • In all of them, Ctrl+Shift+T goes to tape only and back.
