Each chart must say what it is without asking for a click

The root problem: a single bar at the top mixes controls that apply to the tab, to the focused chart and only to the Flow chart, with nothing to tell them apart. The proposal splits this into three decisions.

  1. A fixed line on each chartIt states the bar type and the value. The order-flow chart gets the FLOW badge.
  2. Icons on the chart itself, borderlessA view button that cycles candles and tape, candles only, tape only; the Bubbles, L2, Footprint and Strip layers; and the bubble size as a sample bubble you drag, one above each thing it controls. On is a blue icon; off is grey. Each chart shows only what it accepts.
  3. The top bar holds only what belongs to the tabFeed, symbol, recording, history and orders. Nothing in it depends on clicking a chart first any more.

Only the time charts have a header line. Worse: the layout called "Flow" appears on the time chart on the left, which does not accept the flow layers. The mock-up renames that layout to "Order flow" just to avoid the confusion; the name is yours. Evidence: screenshot, time_header.rs:112.

2The Flow bars disappear

Tick 2000 only appears at the top when Flow is in focus. The app itself admits this in a tooltip: "Click this chart, then use BARS". Evidence: toolbar_wiring.rs:131, time_header.rs:144.

3Four scopes in the same bar

Feed applies to the tab, bars to the focus, three layers to Flow, panel to the window. Nothing in the design separates one from the other. Evidence: toolbar_wiring.rs:104.

4Neighbouring buttons with different targets

Footprint changes the focused chart. Live strip, L2 and bubbles always change Flow, even with another one in focus. No tooltip says so. Evidence: toolbar_wiring.rs:244, toolbar.rs:940.

Proposal: each chart shows its view, its layers and its bars

Clickable. In Flow, click the view button twice, the first one after Tick, to see only the tape: the candles disappear and their layers grey out. The third click goes back. Click Bubbles and slide to L2 to turn both on at once. Drag up the bubble next to the bubbles icon and they grow on the candles; the green bubble at the right end does the same on the tape. Candles and layers are simulated.

Only the tape, without the tick chart

This already exists in the app, named "Tape only (hide candles)", but only through right-click, the checkbox in the bubble settings or by dragging the tape divider to the edge. Nothing on the chart shows that it exists. The panel chose a three-view selector on the chart line.

  • One button that cycles the three views: candles and tape, candles only, tape only, and back. The icon is the view on screen. These are the three states the app already stores (enabled and tape_only). Your choice, among five ways shown in Views of the Flow chart; the cost is not seeing the next view before clicking, and tape-only being two clicks from today's view.
  • Candle layers grey out, they do not vanish: with only the tape, Bubbles, L2 and Footprint turn grey in the same place and keep their state. Going back to candles restores the same look, and the buttons never change position.
  • Dragging becomes a shortcut: dragging the divider to the left lands on "tape only", to the right on "candles only". The 8 px rail ceases to exist, because the chart line never disappears.
  • Keyboard: Ctrl+Shift+T toggles between tape only and the previous view; in the mock-up it works with Flow in focus. It is free today; command orderflow.tape.view.set.

Icons only, and what the visual panel decided

Two agents judged four variants of the line by looking at the images, without tooltips. The visual designer chose V2, borderless and with minimal ink. The outside trader chose V1, with borders, because the border and the text said what was clickable. They agreed on three points, and the final line is V2 with those three.

  • Icons that show the layer: L2, footprint and strip were three identical stacks of lines. Now L2 is a block with three fading bands, footprint is a candle split into two columns of marks, strip is a narrow band against the axis, tape is a column of dots.
  • A clickable signal on the bars: the small arrow on Tick · 2000, at 40% ink. Without it, the outside trader read it as a title.
  • Chart name in words: "FLOW" meant nothing to someone arriving from outside. It became "ORDER FLOW", in small amber capitals, dimmer than the price label.
  • Bubble size, two sample bubbles: you chose the sample bubble, in Bubble scale. The control is a bubble at the current size; drag up and it grows. Since there are two scales, each sample sits above what it controls: the blue one next to the bubbles icon, over the candles; the green one at the right end of the line, over the tape column, in the tape colour. Without the tape, the green one disappears; without candles, the blue one greys out. The value only shows while you drag. Double-click returns to 1×.
  • What was left out: the outside trader asked for words beside each icon. You refused, so the tooltip is the only text, with name and key. He rated confidence per icon at 25 to 60 without words; the new icons should raise that, but it was not measured.

Alternatives I discarded

  • Only a corner menu, on hover: clean, but it hides the buttons and still does not say which chart is which.
  • Legend over the chart, as in TradingView: costs no height, but adds text over the candles, where the indicator list already sits.
  • Only a FLOW label on the top bar: the smallest change. Fixes finding 4 and leaves findings 1 and 2.

Who feels what

  • Scalper: trades the same. Fixed layout and shortcuts intact. The cost is one line less of height in Flow.
  • Context trader: gains more. Reads the timeframe and bars of all charts at once.
  • Newcomer: gains even more. Sees "FLOW" and the view selector. Each icon's tooltip gives the name and the key.

Build order

  • PR 1: fixed line on Flow, badge and bars on all charts. Resolves findings 1 and 2 on its own.
  • PR 2: layer icons, the view button and the two sample bubbles on the line. The two black scale boxes leave the chart. The four icons and the tape chip leave where they are. Resolves finding 4 and exposes tape-only.
  • PR 2b: dragging the divider now lands on the three views; the rail goes away. Saved workspaces with a rail open in "candles and tape".
  • PR 3: switch bars and indicators from the chart line. BARS leaves the top. Resolves finding 3.

Limits of this proposal

  • HTML mock-up. Nothing was tested in the real app with a fast tape. The mock-up does not show the narrow chart, where presets and buttons need to fold into menus.
  • PR 2 changes a written rule: ui-design-model.md §11 says the tape chip sits over the chart. Its reason is still met, because the fixed line does not disappear when the tape is off.
  • Tape-only today forces the native tape (time and execution price, axis fitted to the prints). The panel assumes that is what "only the tape" means; if not, the view button needs a fourth stop.
  • Still to prove in the app: does the Strip draw in tape-only? What happens to drawings and indicators, anchored to a bar, with the candles hidden? How does the line fold on a narrow chart?
  • The menu can read each layer's target from what already exists (LayerScope, tab/panes.rs:307). Control commands and MCP do not change.
